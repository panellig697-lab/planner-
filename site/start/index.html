A few questions before we talk

The first four tell me whether I can help. The rest are the numbers I'd otherwise spend our fifteen minutes asking for — all tap-to-answer, about a minute in total. Then you'll pick a time.

Roughly what's your monthly revenue?

A ballpark is fine — it tells me which flows will move the needle first.

Pick the range closest to where you are.

Are you sending any automated retention emails today?

Flows that fire on their own — carts, welcome, win-back — not one-off campaigns.

Pick whichever is closest — all three are workable.

What are you sending email with now?

Let me know what you're on — "nothing yet" is a fine answer.

When would you want to start?

Even a rough timeline helps.

What's your product focus? (optional)

Now the numbers.

These are the ones I actually build the call around. Estimates are fine, every question has a "not sure", and you can skip any of them.

Roughly how many new customers do you get in a month?
What's a typical order worth?
Roughly what's your gross margin, after cost of goods?
Of the customers who buy once, roughly what share ever buy again?
When someone does come back, do they usually buy once more, or keep buying?
Which of these are running right now? (tick all that apply)
Anything else we should know? (optional)
